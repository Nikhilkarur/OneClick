## 6. Known Edge Cases & System Limitations

* **Catalog gaps.** Software update, Safe mode, Dark mode, auto-rotate, font size, per-app storage and a true Factory data reset have no catalog entry, so those steps resolve to `bixby://dummy_positive` or stay manual. `DL-0022` is the *auto* factory reset, not Factory data reset.
* **Dummy rate by catalog gap.** 22 of 123 gold steps need a Settings screen the catalog lacks, and 14 are physical steps that take no link.
* **Over-cautious links.** 7 steps with a real catalog entry fell back to the placeholder because the match scored under the catalog floor: Settings > Battery > Charging; Settings > Battery and device care > Optimize now; Settings > Display > Navigation bar; Settings > Notifications > Battery percentage; Settings > Security and privacy > Other security settings > Pin windows; Settings > Sounds and vibration > Volume > Alarm; Settings > Sounds and vibration > Volume > Media.
* **Near-miss cache hits.** 18 of 60 near misses were served a cached plan (intent: 16, symptom: 2). The slot guard compares component and symptom only, so a how-do-I request ("I want my screen to go black after a minute") matches the cached fault report with the same words; an empty cached symptom also matches any symptom.
* **Weak paraphrase registers.** typo 40% hit rate with the cache warmed on the original phrasing only.
* **Not measured yet:** step accuracy (`judge.py`); cold-path latency and cost over HTTP (`loadtest.py --mode api`); schema and rule compliance on non-empty plans (`gate_replica.py`); the LLM mapping baseline (`ablation.py`, needs `MISTRAL_API_KEY`).
